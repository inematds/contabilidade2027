How it works

Five parts, no jargon

📅 How the month went

How much was sold, how much was left over, and the margin, versus the previous month and versus the average.

🧾 Line by line

Sales, minus cost, minus expenses, minus taxes, equals the month's profit: the bakery accounting that the owner understands. Profit is before partner withdrawals and installments.

💰 Cash flow

The balance, the real pace at which it rises or falls, the projection for the next 3 months and, with accounts entered, how much comes in and goes out over the next 30 days. For Lucro Presumido and Lucro Real, the reserve for split payment.

🚨 Alerts

Margin 3 points or more below average, out-of-pattern expense, customer owing money, short or negative cash, and the most useful one: when the result shows a surplus but cash drops. It could be a customer paying later, inventory, a supplier paid early, owner withdrawals, an installment or an investment, and the dashboard tells you to check.

✅ What to do

3 actions for the month, from most to least urgent: collect from those who owe you, build a reserve, review expenses.

🔍 Verified numbers

If the AI rewrites the text, the kit's checker blocks any value that didn't come from the data. It doesn't catch a change of meaning (went up instead of went down) or a forgotten alert: your own read before sending is still necessary.

⚠️ What it is NOT

  • It is not a trial balance or a tax assessment. It's the month's readout for the owner, built from the numbers you've already closed.
  • The projection is simple: repeats the average change in the balance over recent months. It doesn't account for new sales, seasonality, or cuts.
  • It doesn't send anything on its own. You review and send it.

Where it comes from

The numbers come of your monthly close

📒 What you already have

The dashboard uses the client's monthly summary: revenue, costs, expenses, taxes paid, and balance. No new data to make up.

🧾 Split payment

The reserve for split payment is calculated with the same rule as the Diagnostic. Rollout is gradual and depends on an act by the IBS Steering Committee (made up of states and municipalities) and the Federal Revenue Service (LC 214, art. 35). The amount is a ceiling, based on the 11.3% scenario and without deducting credits. For Simples companies, the dashboard does not calculate this reserve.

Common questions

Before you start

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You paste the request and the AI does the rest.

How much does it cost?

The kit and this guide are free. The easiest path uses Claude Code or Codex, which come with their paid subscription.

Can I send it over WhatsApp?

It works: the prompt already tells the AI to rewrite the dashboard as a short text. The checker blocks any number that did not come from the data; you check for a change in meaning (said "rose" instead of "fell") when you read it.

What if I don't have accounts receivable and payable?

The dashboard comes out without the 30-day buffer and asks you to enter it the following month. Everything else works the same.
