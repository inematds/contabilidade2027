How it works

Three pieces, no jargon

📁 The client folder

É o client data checklist: a spreadsheet summarizing each month (revenue, costs, taxes paid, and balance) and a few answers about the business, such as its tax regime and average collection period. Expenses by type and product prices are optional and make the diagnosis more detailed.

🧮 The assessment

É a a calculator that doesn’t forget anything. It does the same calculations, the same way, for every client, and records where each number came from. It doesn’t send anything to the internet.

🔍 The checker

É o footer checker. After the AI rewrites the report in plain language, it checks that every amount in reais, percentage, and deadline came from the data. If the AI rounds it to “around 60,000,” it blocks it.

📄 What’s included in the 2 pages

1. What’s left over

What sold, what was left over, the margin, and whether the margin fell over the last 3 months.

2. Your cash flow

How many months of expenses cash can cover, and which months will be tight.

3. 2027 tax reform SIMULATED

Today’s PIS/Cofins × CBS/IBS in two scenarios, the effect of split payment, and, if they sell to businesses, a note that those businesses may use the tax as a credit.

4. What stood out

The expense that strayed from the average and the product with a tight margin, with the target price.

5. Your 3 priorities

From highest value to lowest, with a 30-minute meeting agenda.

⚠️ What it is NOT

  • It’s not an opinion letter. It's a simulation with assumptions stated in the report. You, the accountant, make the decision and sign off.
  • It doesn’t know the final rate. The Senate sets the reference rate by 12/15/2026, the standard rate from which each activity starts (with or without reduction). That is why the kit uses two scenarios and marks 2027 as SIMULATED. Redo it when it comes out.
  • It doesn’t change the Simples DAS. For those using Simples, the report alerts you to the credit available to business customers, but it doesn’t simulate the DAS.
  • It doesn’t send anything to the client. You review and present it.

Where it comes from

The 2027 rules come of the law; the kit is ours

The calculations follow the Tax Reform law and the official figures available as of 24/11/2026. The kit (program, examples, and text) belongs to INEMA and is not affiliated with the Receita.

📜 The Tax Reform law

Complementary Law 214/2025. In 2027, CBS is the benchmark rate minus 0.1 percentage point (art. 347), and IBS is 0.05% state + 0.05% municipal (art. 344). That’s why, in the kit, the two add up to the benchmark rate itself. Split payment is phased in gradually (art. 35).

Text of LC 214 in the Senate

⚖️ The two scenarios

8,8% is just the value used in the official example of the Receita simulator, not a proposal. 11,3% is the reference rate calculated by the TCU on 10/28/2026. The Senate sets the final one by 12/15/2026. Using both shows the client a range, not a single number.

TCU note

🏪 The Simples regime

Those using Simples can pay CBS and IBS outside the DAS to give business customers full credit. The choice is made twice a year, in September and March.

LC 227/2026 in the Chamber of Deputies

Common questions

Before you get started

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You paste the prompt and the AI does the rest. The commands are in the README, if you prefer.

How much does it cost?

The kit and this guide are free. The easiest way to use them is with Claude Code or Codex, which are included with a paid Claude or ChatGPT subscription. Anyone who knows how to run a Python program can use it without AI.

Does it work for any tax regime?

Lucro Presumido and Lucro Real have the CBS/IBS simulation. Simples has the cash flow, the margin, and the notice about customers' credit. MEI and cases with their own rules (fuels, monophasic, tax substitution, specific regimes) are left out: for those, use the kit only for cash flow and margin.

And what if the activity has a reduced tax rate?

You enter the reduction (30% or 60%, depending on the activity), and the kit recalculates everything. Healthcare and education, for example, qualify for a reduction.

Can I charge for the assessment?

Yes. The kit is open source (MIT license). The service, review, and technical responsibility are yours.

Can the result be used for filing?

No. It’s a simulation to support discussion and decision-making. Tax filing is still done in your system.
