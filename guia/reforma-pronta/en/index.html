How it works

Four checks, no jargon

🏷️ The registration, item by item

É o form reviewer. Checks that each item has its codes and that they are in the right format: NCM with 8 digits, NBS with 9, CST with 3, and cClassTrib with 6. It also checks that the cClassTrib starts with the CST, as in the official examples, and that the rate reduction matches the CST: a reduction with CST 000 (full taxation), CST 200 (reduced rate) with no reduction, or a reduction with no cClassTrib. A reduction outside 30%, 60%, or 100% becomes a "check this", not an error: there are specific regimes with other ranges.

📊 What weighs the most

É a queue by importance. It ranks the items by sales and shows the few that make up 80% of sales. A pending issue there matters more than on an item that hardly sells.

🤝 The suppliers

É o credit map. Shows how much of your purchases come from Simples or MEI suppliers. On those purchases, the CBS and IBS credit is limited to what the supplier pays under Simples; for MEI, which pays a fixed amount, it tends to be almost nothing.

✅ The checklist

There are 6 yes-or-no questions: the system has the new fields, a test invoice has already been issued, the registry was reviewed, the contracts have a pass-through clause, prices were reviewed, and the team knows what changes.

⚠️ What it is NOT

  • It doesn't say whether the code is the right one for the item. It checks the format and consistency. You are the one who confirms the classification, using the official table or the test in the open calculator from the Receita (INEMA kit).
  • It doesn't issue invoices or touch the system. It just reads the list and builds the plan.
  • It is not a formal opinion. The priorities are ranked suggestions; the decision is yours.

Where it comes from

The rules come of the law and of the official examples

📜 Credit on purchases

Complementary Law 214/2025, art. 47: purchases from a Simples supplier generate credit, limited to what the supplier pays under Simples.

Text of LC 214 in the Senate

🏪 Simples election

LC 123, art. 13, §§ 9 and 10, as worded by LC 227/2026: a Simples company can pay CBS and IBS under the regular regime. The option is semiannual, made in September and in March.

LC 227/2026 in the Chamber of Deputies

🧮 Codes and official engine

The CST/cClassTrib pair follows the pattern of the examples in the Receita's open calculator (CST 000 with 000001; CST 200 with 200003, 200028, 200034). To check the calculation item by item, use the official engine's test.
